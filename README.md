## How it works

Every page is a standalone HTML file with its CSS and JavaScript inline. There is no build step and no dependencies. Open `index.html` in a browser, or run `ruby server.rb` to serve the folder locally.

The Paper 2 trackers save each student's ticks, ratings and notes to that browser's local storage. Nothing is transmitted anywhere and nothing is shared between students or devices. Clearing site data clears a student's work, so the trackers include an export button.

## Assessment details

Assessment information follows the IB Language A guide for first teaching September 2024 and first assessment May 2026. Paper 2 is a comparative essay of 1 hour 45 minutes, closed book, marked out of 25 across five criteria of five marks each: A knowledge, understanding and interpretation; B1 analysis and evaluation of textual features and broader authorial choices; B2 comparative analysis; C focus and organization; D language.

## A note on the Paper 2 trackers

Comparative material in the trackers is deliberately locked until the partner work has been taught. Students cannot compare a work they have not yet studied, so pairings, comparative hooks and Criterion B2 are shown as locked panels rather than previewed.

## Text and quotations

Quotations from works still in copyright are given briefly and always accompanied by commentary, for the purposes of study and criticism. *Antigone* is quoted in a translation-neutral form; wording will differ between editions and students are asked to name their translator.
